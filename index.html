first time opening it on your Mac?
  1. Unzip the download and drag Pawmodoro into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can’t check the app for malicious software, because it isn’t from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Look for the paw in your menu bar. Click it to show or hide your cat.
